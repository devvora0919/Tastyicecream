Small shop, big scoops.

We started Tasty Ice-Cream to give our neighbourhood in Bopal a friendly spot to unwind in the evenings — real cream, real fruit, and flavors that taste like they should.

Every tub is made in small batches, so what you get is fresh, never watery, and always generous.
